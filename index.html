About Our Drink

Vouke is a beverage brand that makes energy drinks with coconut water, green tea, matcha, and collagen. The drinks come in flavors such as Blueberry Coconut, Mango Coconut, Peach Coconut, and Coconut. According to product information, a 12-ounce can of some Vouke energy drinks contains 80 mg of caffeine and zero grams of sugar. The brand focuses on combining tropical flavors with ingredients used in energy drinks. Vouke also gained recognition through the H-EB Quest for Texas Best competition and appeared on Shark Tank in Latin America
